## 2. Payment Terms & Financial Policy

* **Pay After Live:** Standard single and bulk orders ($12–$15/post) operate on a **"pay after live"** policy—you process payment only after reviewing the published article and links.
* **Advance Floor Rate:** The discounted **$10/post rate** is strictly available for bulk orders (5+ articles) paid upfront.
* **Accepted Payment Methods:** Wise, Payoneer, or direct Bank Transfer.
* **Non-Negotiable Baseline:** **$10 per placement** is our absolute operational floor rate to cover engineering team code/technical review and hosting infrastructure.

## 3. Editorial Standards & Link Policy

All submissions undergo a technical evaluation by our engineering team to ensure alignment with our site standards:

* **Accepted Niches:** Strictly Software Engineering, SaaS Architecture, AI/ML Workflows, Cloud/DevOps, or Web/Mobile Development.
* **Content Requirements:** Minimum **1,000+ words**, deeply technical, highly actionable, and **100% human-written** (AI-generated filler or surface-level marketing content will be rejected).
* **Link Allowance:** Up to **1–2 permanent DoFollow links** per post placed naturally in the body text.
* **Publishing Style:** Clean, organic publication with **zero "sponsored" or advertorial tags**, plus a dedicated author profile page.
* **Link Insertion Policy:** Incoming backlinks are published exclusively through new guest articles. Link insertions into past/existing posts are strictly unavailable.

---

## 4. Prohibited Niches & Categories

We enforce a strict **zero-tolerance policy** against non-technical or high-risk content. We do not accept guest posts, links, or pitches from the following categories under any circumstances or price points:

* Casino, Gambling, iGaming, or Sports Betting
* CBD, Cannabis, Vape, or Pharmaceuticals
* Cryptocurrency, Forex, Trading, or High-Risk Finance
* Adult Content, Essay Writing Services, or General Non-Technical Topics

## 5. Turnaround Time (TAT) & Workflow

1. **Pitch / Submission:** Share 2–3 technical topic pitches alongside target URLs and anchor text, or submit a completed draft to [bridgehomies.com/blog/submit](https://www.bridgehomies.com/blog/submit).
2. **Technical Review & Publishing:** **2–3 business days** turnaround time for technical review, code block formatting, and live site deployment.
3. **Verification & Settlement:** Live article link sent to client $\rightarrow$ Client verifies DoFollow placement $\rightarrow$ Payment processed via Wise / Payoneer / Bank Transfer (or reciprocal link verification for $0 exchanges).
