faq:
- question: Why might a marketplace still reject a listing after I change the reported field?
  answer: The reported field may not show where the mismatch began. Check the source product record, category mapping, feed or connector status, and the destination’s current validation details.
- question: How can I find which system reported a listing error?
  answer: Review the message location and available product or feed identifiers in your store, connector, feed tool, and marketplace account. Follow the error to the system that issued it; the exact workflow varies by setup.
- question: What are common categories of listing errors?
  answer: Issues can involve product data, catalog structure, feed synchronization, marketplace rules, account permissions, or technical integrations. See the guide to six marketplace listing error types for examples.
- question: Why change one relevant field at a time?
  answer: Keeping a record of each change makes it easier to compare the next validation result with the prior state. Where practical, test one relevant change at a time so you can identify which change may have affected the result.
- question: How long does a marketplace take to reprocess a listing?
  answer: Processing time varies by destination, connector, and review state. Check the status information and current guidance for the specific marketplace or feed path before resubmitting or making additional changes.
- question: What information should I collect before escalating a listing issue?
  answer: Collect the product or feed identifier, the exact validation message, relevant timestamps, recent changes, and available processing logs or screenshots. Share only information requested through the marketplace’s supported escalation channel.
