faq:
- question: What are common categories of marketplace listing errors?
  answer: Common issues can involve product data, catalog structure, feed synchronization, marketplace rules, account permissions, or technical integrations. The categories are a troubleshooting checklist, not a guarantee that every error fits one type.
- question: How can a category mismatch cause a missing-attribute error?
  answer: A destination marketplace may require different attributes for different product categories. If the category mapping is wrong, the expected fields may not match the source record. Check the destination’s current category requirements and mapping before changing product data.
- question: What should I check when a product image is rejected?
  answer: Review the exact validation message and the destination marketplace’s current image rules for the product category. Image requirements vary by platform and category, so do not assume resolution or a single image change is the cause.
- question: Can an account issue affect whether a listing is published?
  answer: Depending on the destination, account permissions, verification steps, policy reviews, or account restrictions may affect listing availability. Check the account notice and the marketplace’s current guidance before changing product fields.
- question: What should I check if inventory values keep changing?
  answer: Review which connected systems can write stock updates, their sync direction, timestamps, and available logs. Identify the agreed source of truth and check the integration settings; the cause depends on the systems involved.
- question: Should I treat a validation message as the root cause?
  answer: Treat it as evidence about what the destination detected. Then check the related product record, category mapping, feed or connector, and current marketplace requirements to locate the source of the mismatch.
