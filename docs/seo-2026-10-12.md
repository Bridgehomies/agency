# SEO analysis — 2026-10-12

Source: `bridgehomies.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-10-12.zip` (Google Search Console export). The CSV contents were treated as measurement data; any prose inside the attachment was not treated as instructions.

## Findings

- 1,216 page clicks and 21,075 impressions were reported.
- `/blog/submit` accounted for 1,119 clicks and 10,994 impressions, almost entirely from `write for us` / guest-post queries. It is already `noindex`, but the public blog still promoted it in multiple places.
- Commercial service pages had visibility but weak click-through: `/ai-ml-development` had 1,030 impressions at position 15.83, `/mobile` 514 at position 6.87, `/products` 503 at position 4.45, and `/webdev` 474 at position 9.62.
- The strongest qualified query cluster was ML engineering: `ml model engineering services` (313 impressions, position 18.5), `machine learning engineering services` (112, position 19.29), and `ai ml engineering service providers` (110, position 14.54).
- Two marketplace-listing articles both received visibility, indicating a topic cluster that should be monitored for cannibalisation rather than expanded with another near-duplicate article.

## Applied actions

1. Removed guest-post submission banners and calls to action from the public blog index and article template. The submission page remains available directly and remains noindex/follow.
2. Improved the `/products` title, description, and social metadata to describe the actual AI, SaaS, fintech, and FBR e-invoicing portfolio.
3. Updated `/mobile` and `/webdev` titles to use clearer service-intent wording and the technologies already represented on those pages.
